Machine Learning Rigor & Review Defense
Dynamic Selection

The system does not blindly hardcode Random Forest as the superior algorithm. The best model is selected dynamically using harmonic mean (F1-score) and test accuracy.

Leakage Prevention

StandardScaler is embedded inside Scikit-learn Pipelines. Parameters ($\mu, \sigma$) are learned strictly on the training partition and transformed on test sets.

Sample Size Integrity

Small datasets are flagged with caution banners. Illustrative demonstration data is clearly distinguished from real institutional data to maintain scientific integrity.
